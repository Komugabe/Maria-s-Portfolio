## About Me

I am a Geospatial Data Scientist, University Lecturer, and Research Fellow working at the intersection of spatial analytics, artificial intelligence, healthcare logistics, and higher education pedagogy. My career spans academic research, university instruction, and international curriculum consulting across the United States and Uganda.

My primary work focuses on bridging the gap between advanced spatial modeling and real-world health outcomes — developing AI- and GIS-driven decision-support frameworks to optimize medical supply distribution, predict climate-driven disease patterns, and enhance primary healthcare delivery in low-income settings.
