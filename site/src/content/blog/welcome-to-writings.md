Most of what a research paper contains is the *result*. This is a place for the
rest of it — the method choices that didn't fit in a paper, notes from fieldwork and
conferences, and the occasional reflection on building tools that move real health
resources in Uganda.

Posts here are short and infrequent by design. Expect a handful a year, usually
tied to a paper, a talk, or a partnership milestone.
