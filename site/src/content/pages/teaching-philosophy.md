---
title: Teaching philosophy
---

Maria teaches through projects, practical examples, and professional
communication — helping students translate technical findings into
organizational recommendations using Python, SPSS, and applied business
scenarios. Student feedback consistently recognizes her ability to explain
complex programming and information-systems concepts clearly, to foster a
welcoming and inclusive learning environment, and to support students'
technical confidence, persistence, and career preparation across in-person,
online, and hybrid formats.
