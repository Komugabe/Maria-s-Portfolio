title: About bio

Maria's path runs between two places. It began in Uganda, where she taught
information technology, systems analysis, and computer studies at the
University of Kisubi from 2018 — supervising capstone research and helping
students build technical confidence — work later recognized with a University
of Kisubi Award of Appreciation for IT skilling.

She came to Claremont Graduate University for a master's in Information Systems
& Technology, concentrating in GIS, and stayed for a Ph.D. Her doctoral
research integrated AI and GIS for climate-driven malaria monitoring,
demand-based resource distribution, and supply optimization in low-income
countries. At commencement in May 2026 she carried the CIST department banner.

Alongside her degree, she has served since 2023 as curriculum consultant and
institutional liaison for the Harvey Mudd College Clinic–Musizi University
partnership — mentoring three student teams building computing and software
engineering curricula for Uganda's first private liberal arts university,
translating between U.S. and Ugandan academic and regulatory expectations.

Today she lectures at California State University, San Bernardino, holds a
research fellowship at CGU's Center for Information Systems & Technology,
and continues to build decision-support tools that move real health resources
to where they are needed most.
