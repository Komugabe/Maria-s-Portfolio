abstract: >-
  This study explores the use of geospatial analysis to address regional
  disparities in low birth weight (LBW) rates across California. By creating
  a spatial visualization tool, the research highlights the socioeconomic
  and environmental factors influencing LBW such as income, healthcare
  access and education. The tool provides policymakers and healthcare
  providers with actionable insights to inform targeted interventions and is
  evaluated by public-health subject-matter experts for usability and
  effectiveness.
