abstract: >-
  Describes the ongoing collaboration between Harvey Mudd College (Claremont,
  CA) and Musizi University (Kampala, Uganda) — Uganda's first private
  liberal arts university. Novel aspects include the long-standing
  international collaboration and the focus on student-led curriculum
  development, positioned within HMC's undergraduate capstone (Clinic)
  program. The report reflects on the challenges and opportunities of this
  unique capstone structure.
