abstract: >-
  The study examined factors affecting student achievement in computer
  studies in the Uganda Certificate of Education Examinations (UCE),
  conducted in secondary schools across Katabi Sub-County, Wakiso District,
  Uganda. Using a correlational design with 138 purposively selected student
  respondents across four secondary schools, the study found a moderate
  negative correlation (r = −0.655, p = .001) between teacher qualification
  and student performance, a moderate negative correlation (r = −0.580,
  p = .001) between teacher experience and student grades, and a moderate
  negative correlation (r = −0.393, p = .001) between the number of students
  per computer and student grades. Findings suggest that, beyond certain
  levels, these factors may not directly enhance student achievement,
  highlighting the need to consider other elements such as teaching quality
  and resource utilisation.
