abstract: >-
  Prompted by Covid-19's disruption of university education in Uganda, this
  quantitative cross-sectional study investigated student and lecturer
  perceptions of e-learning effectiveness in private and public
  universities. Two-sample t-tests revealed significant differences in
  perceptions between students (p < 0.05) and between lecturers (p < 0.05)
  at private vs. public universities, better explained by institutional
  readiness to adopt e-learning. The study recommends universities acquire
  the necessary ICT infrastructure, make e-learning easily accessible, and
  continuously train students and lecturers in e-learning methodologies.
